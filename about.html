About ProxiPro

The platform trusted relationships deserve.

ProxiPro was built on a simple observation: the most valuable professional introductions in real estate don't come from algorithms or ad spend. They come from relationships built on trust, maintained over time, and shared between people who have already earned each other's confidence.

Our Mission

Give professional relationships a permanent place to live.

Every home purchase creates a web of trusted connections — agents, lenders, insurance professionals, inspectors, contractors, and service providers — each one carefully chosen and personally vouched for.

Those relationships don't end when the transaction does. But without a platform built to support them, they fade. Phone numbers get lost. Business cards disappear. The professionals who earned a homeowner's trust become hard to find when they're needed most.

ProxiPro exists to change that. We give every trusted professional connection the infrastructure to stay organized, visible, and valuable — long after closing day and long into the lifetime of the home.

The Problem We're Solving

Trusted relationships are everywhere. A platform for them isn't.

The home and property industry runs on referrals and reputation. But the infrastructure supporting those relationships hasn't kept pace with how professionals actually work.

📱

Relationships live in text threads

Trusted connections are scattered across texts, emails, business cards, and memory. When a homeowner needs a roofer at 11pm, they shouldn't have to dig through years of messages to find the one their agent recommended.

🔄

Relationships reset after closing

Agents, lenders, and insurance professionals spend months earning trust — then that relationship goes dormant the moment the transaction or renewal is complete. ProxiPro keeps those connections active and useful.

🏆

Great work deserves better reach

Service providers who deliver excellent work still compete against strangers on price and speed. ProxiPro puts the best providers inside the networks of the professionals who already know and trust their work.

What We Believe

The real estate industry runs on trust. It always has.

The best real estate agents don't just close transactions — they become a trusted resource for life. The best lenders don't disappear at closing — they stay connected because that's how lasting relationships work. The best contractors don't compete on price alone — they earn the recommendation of someone who already matters to the client.

ProxiPro does not change the way business is done. It gives that business the platform it deserves — one that keeps trusted relationships visible, organized, and valuable across the full lifetime of every home.

We built ProxiPro because we believe the strength of a professional's network should reflect the strength of their reputation — and every homeowner deserves access to professionals they can trust before they ever need to pick up the phone.

The real asset isn't the transaction. It's the relationship.
